1. Start.
2. Initialize all counts and totals to zero.
3. Read an expense amount.
4. If the amount is 0, go to Step 9.
5. If the amount is negative, display an error and read the next amount.
6. Increase the valid expense count and grand total.
7. Classify the expense:
   - Less than ₹100: Small expense.
   - ₹100 to less than ₹500: Medium expense.
   - ₹500 or more: Large expense.
8. Repeat Steps 3–7.
9. Display the count and total for each category, grand total, and average.
10. Stop.
